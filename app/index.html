Assumptions & exclusions

Buoyed weight is estimated from component linear mass and a simplified buoyancy factor. Axial load is integrated bottom-up. Normal force is approximated from transverse gravity and changes in inclination/azimuth; friction is μN in the direction of movement. Bending stresses, buckling, distributed contact forces, viscous losses, motor torque and realistic bit reactions are excluded.
