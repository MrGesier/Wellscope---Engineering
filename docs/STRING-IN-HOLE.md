# Whole-string transverse contact preview

Open `#string-in-hole` or BHA static > Whole string in hole. The original synthetic Northbank example contains a full 3,800 m tally, survey, casing/open-hole architecture and assumed steel stiffness. It does not load or overwrite the current project's engineering inputs. Export its JSON to see the complete schema; import a complete sourced study to replace it. Joint dimensions in the example are assumptions, not vendor ratings.

Run in / Pull out changes bit MD and recalculates static equilibrium every 700 ms. Travel step is a visualization step, not a physical running speed. Disable Follow bit to watch components pass a fixed well interval. Select a component or contact for inspection. Surface triangles show schematic body features and repeated pipe joints; beam stations are a separate calculation mesh. Arbitrary imported equipment gets an equivalent tube unless its family is recognized. No manufacturer CAD accuracy is claimed.

The optional **Distributed loads → 3D shape** panel now supplies a one-way soft-string effective tension profile and sourced axial/torsional limit checks. See [STRING_LOAD_COUPLING.md](STRING_LOAD_COUPLING.md) for its boundary assumptions and exclusions. Constant tension remains the default.

## Formulation and limitations
Two transverse unknowns per survey station, in fixed local normal planes. Minimize discrete bending energy EI |p[i-1]-2p[i]+p[i+1]|^2/(2h^3), prescribed constant-tension energy T |p[i+1]-p[i]|^2/(2h), and buoyed gravity potential, subject to circular clearance disks. Both endpoints are centred pins. Equivalent EI is harmonically averaged in cells spanning components. Contact diameters and bore clearance conservatively cover each cell; short features may therefore affect nearby stations. Repeated joint envelopes are modeled where cells intersect declared joint intervals; the preflight interference check conservatively checks maximum joint OD throughout the component.

Banded Cholesky rejects an indefinite free system before a projected Newton contact solve. Active disks retain tangent motion and their contact multiplier contributes constraint curvature; an energy line search and projected relaxation fallback preserve feasibility. Maximum projected equilibrium residual must be below 0.5 N. Transverse displacement gradients above 0.1 are rejected. Reactions are lumped nodal reactions, and depend on mesh spacing: they are not distributed contact pressures. Compare 5 / 10 / 20 m meshes; local tool contacts need refinement beyond this whole-well preview. Uniform input is limited to 1,000 intervals; the adaptive mesh is limited to 2,000 intervals.

The inspection view straightens the survey for readable local offsets; overview shows actual survey geometry. Transverse magnification is reported and shared by bore and string. Red marks show contact envelopes, which may exceed a rendered body at a coarse station. Surface and bit supports are not wall contacts. No nonlinear postbuckling, transverse-friction feedback, torsional deformation, vibrations, wear or cuttings transport is solved. Constant tension is an independent trial input, not inferred from hookload/WOB. This is a preliminary visualization model, not a validated stiff-string operating-window calculation.

## Verification
Analytical simply supported gravity beam deflection within 1%, zero transverse load for a vertical beam, rotation invariance, tensile stiffening, circular clearance constraints, rejection of unstable compression and geometric interference, and full-string motion across casing shoes. Browser checks cover both travel directions, tool selection, camera invariance, invalidation after input edits and mobile rendering.

## 3D and design comparison update

The local 3D scene follows the minimum-curvature survey geometry. Its WebGL renderer supports orbit, pan, zoom, cutaway/transparent/hidden bore, surface wireframe, beam nodes and a shared bending-stress scale for A/B. Imported unknown tool families remain equivalent tubes. The cross-section displays actual transverse proportions at the nearest solved station; dashed contact envelopes can be larger than the body because the solver conservatively covers the cell. The older 2D inspection remains available if WebGL is unavailable.

The solver now evaluates each survey interval as a circular minimum-curvature arc, rather than linearly interpolating coordinates and introducing artificial corners. Adaptive stations include all component and architecture interfaces; the bottom 120 m additionally resolves tool-joint boundaries and refines to the local target spacing. At least three intervals resolve each bottom anchor span. The upper drillpipe retains coarse, conservative joint contact envelopes. This is feature-based refinement, not an automatic error-estimating adaptive method. Nonuniform bending uses 2 EI/(hLeft+hRight) times the squared slope jump; nodal gravity uses the actual control-volume length.

Keep A freezes a complete in-memory input/result snapshot. Edit a selected component's length, body/contact diameters, mass or equivalent E for B. A/B only displays when survey, architecture, bit MD, mud density, constant tension and mesh settings match. Station locations can still differ with geometry. Stress colours use the same scale. Export includes both complete inputs, metrics and qualification; snapshots are not automatically persisted across reloads. A failed calculation clears B's shape. Draft component edits are labelled unapplied until Apply.

The refinement control halves the upper mesh spacing (minimum 1 m) and local target spacing (minimum 0.1 m), reporting differences in maximum eccentricity, peak sampled bending and summed wall reaction magnitudes. It does not declare convergence automatically. Validate both designs; a converged numerical solution does not establish field validity.
