# Concrete well workflow

The default route is Prepare the well: trajectory, bore architecture, lithology and existing tally or a generated starting geometry. Six navigation entries replace the previous catalogue. Old modules retain their data, direct routes and contextual access; they are not deleted.

A prepared drilling programme supplies the section dashboard and the explicit 3D inspection action. The independent BHA builder and detailed load workspace still require explicit imports. Project data must be exported to retain a backup; preparing a study is not a durable filesystem save.

The terracotta synoptic uses measured depth and projected radial displacement, with interpreted lithological intervals. It is not a geological cross-section. Clicking a layer or moving the depth control evaluates that depth. Unlogged gaps and absent bounds remain unknown.

A generated BHA uses assumed geometry, steel density and stiffness, and requested measurement families. It has no OEM ratings or verified thread compatibility. Existing contact and bending models compare discrete placement candidates at sampled depths. The selected eligible Pareto candidate minimizes summed wall reaction with bending as a tie-breaker. This is neither a globally optimal BHA nor a proof against sticking between sampled stations.

WOB, RPM, flow and ROP are entered targets, not prescriptions inferred from lithology. Bounds require a source. Pickup/slackoff and rotating torque use the off-bottom load model. Directional response and pressure profiles require their own associated study and conditions; no generic SPP-to-build law or WOB-to-ROP prediction is introduced.

Editing inputs invalidates visible results. Operating-target-only edits preserve sourced intervals; a geometry rebuild drops associated directional and hydraulic profiles. Changed 3D geometry also invalidates the dashboard, while inspection-depth movement does not.
